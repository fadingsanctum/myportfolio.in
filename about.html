Who I am

Learning by doing.

Hey! I'm Shaik Muzammil — a student developer from Guntur, India who got into coding about a year ago and hasn't looked back since. I mostly self-taught myself through YouTube, documentation, and just breaking things and figuring out how to fix them.

In the past year I've built 9 projects ranging from silly satirical websites to more serious apps like a digital library. Each one was a learning experience — some went well, some were messy, but all of them taught me something I didn't know before.

How I Think

Things I Believe

01

Build Things, Don't Just Watch

Tutorials are great for a while, but you only really learn by building your own stuff and breaking it. Most of what I know came from making something and debugging it.

02

Be Honest About What You Don't Know

I'm a student. I don't know everything, and that's fine. Pretending otherwise just wastes everyone's time. I'd rather ask a question and learn than fake it.

03

Ship It, Then Improve

Waiting for something to be perfect before sharing it means you never ship anything. I'd rather get it out there, get feedback, and make it better over time.
